## Notes
- Search, sort and filter state live in `lists/list-query.ts`, `use-list-query.ts` and `suggestion-source.ts`
  and the components `ListSearchBar`, `ListSearchField`, `ColumnHeading`, `ColumnFilter` know nothing of tasks.
  The case list (S13) passes its own columns (`QueryableColumn`) and its own suggestion loader.
- Defaults taken: the reset button clears the search and the column filters but keeps the sort; the
  counts of the toggle and of the primary filters stay those of the whole list (a search does not
  change them, and the first page of a narrowed list does not overwrite them); suggestions respect
  the column filters but not the full-text input; a filter on a person column uses `<path>.sort`;
  date columns are sortable but not filterable (the server matches text, not dates).
- Backend: new test `TheListsAreSearchedFilteredAndSortedTest` (sort by `title.de`, `workflowTitle.de`,
  `assignee.sort,assignee.id`, filters, plain-text terms, OPTIONS suggestions). No change to the backend code.
